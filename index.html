The rule: weeks run Monday to Sunday. 2026 and 2027 each keep their own numbering — 2027 restarts at Week 1. No week is allowed to run from one year into the next, so days that fall outside a complete Monday–Sunday week are shown as year-open days and year-close days.
Rule

How a Year Divides Into Weeks

Common Year — 2026 & 2027 365 days = 52 weeks + 1 day
Leap Year — not in this range 366 days = 52 weeks + 2 days
What Happens To The Leftover Day Because a week may not cross into the next year, the leftover day cannot simply be tacked onto a 53rd week. It is absorbed by the calendar’s alignment and appears in the ledger as opening days before Week 1 and a short final week at year-end. Both years below are common years (365 days).
